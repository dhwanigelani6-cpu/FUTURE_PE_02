
# UGC Ad Content Pack – Velvet Glow

## 1. Campaign Overview
Product: Velvet Matte Lipstick
Brand: Velvet Glow (fictional)
Platforms: Instagram Reels, Facebook, YouTube Shorts
Style: Conversational, creator-inspired product advertising
Goal: Product awareness and audience engagement

## 2. Five Marketing Hooks

1. "Let's find inspiration for your next makeup look!"
2. "Come with me for a quick lipstick product reveal."
3. "Which lipstick shade would you choose?"
4. "A simple product reveal for your beauty-content inspiration."
5. "Let's take a closer look at Velvet Glow."

## 3. Instagram Reels Script – 25 Seconds

Scene 1 (0–3 sec):
Visual: Lipstick package on a clean background.
Voiceover: "Looking for your next makeup inspiration?"

Scene 2 (3–7 sec):
Visual: Open the package and reveal the lipstick.
Voiceover: "Meet the Velvet Matte Lipstick concept."

Scene 3 (7–13 sec):
Visual: Show the packaging and lipstick in close-up.
Voiceover: "Let's explore the details of this beauty-inspired product."

Scene 4 (13–19 sec):
Visual: Display the lipstick beside makeup accessories.
Voiceover: "Which shade would you pair with your everyday look?"

Scene 5 (19–25 sec):
Visual: Show the final product frame.
Text: "Velvet Glow – Discover Your Shade."
CTA: "Explore the collection."

## 4. Facebook Ad

Headline:
Discover the Velvet Matte Lipstick Concept

Primary Text:
Looking for fresh beauty inspiration? Explore the Velvet Glow lipstick concept through a simple product showcase. Discover the visual details and share which shade you would choose for your next makeup look.

CTA:
Explore the Collection

## 5. YouTube Shorts Script – 20 Seconds

0–3 sec:
Hook: "Let's create a lipstick product reveal!"

3–8 sec:
Show the package and reveal the lipstick.

8–14 sec:
Use close-up shots with soft lighting.

14–20 sec:
Display the product name and ask viewers to share their favourite shade.

CTA:
"Discover Your Shade."

## 6. Problem–Solution–CTA Format

Problem:
Choosing a shade for a makeup look can feel overwhelming.

Solution:
Explore the Velvet Glow lipstick concept and use its visual presentation for makeup inspiration.

CTA:
"Explore the collection and choose your favourite shade."

## 7. Social Media Captions

Instagram:
A little lipstick inspiration for your next makeup look. 💄 Which shade would you choose?

Facebook:
Discover our fictional Velvet Glow lipstick concept and share your favourite shade idea.

YouTube Shorts:
A closer look at a lipstick-inspired beauty concept. Which shade is your pick?

## 8. CTA Variations
- Explore the collection.
- Discover your shade.
- Find your next makeup inspiration.
- Take a closer look.
- Share your favourite shade.

## 9. Brand Voice
Friendly, conversational, positive, and simple.

Use short sentences and visual storytelling. Avoid fake testimonials, invented discounts, and unsupported cosmetic claims.

## 10. Expected Outcome
A collection of reusable hooks, scripts, captions, and CTAs that can be adapted for different social media platforms.
