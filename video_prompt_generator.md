
# AI Video Prompt Generator – Velvet Glow

## Purpose
To create a short, professional lipstick advertisement using an AI video-generation tool such as Google Flow.

## Main Prompt

Create a premium, realistic product advertisement for a fictional lipstick brand named Velvet Glow.

Video format: Vertical 9:16.
Duration: Approximately 20–25 seconds.
Style: Modern beauty commercial with creator-inspired storytelling.
Lighting: Soft studio lighting.
Background: Clean and elegant.
Camera: Smooth zooms, close-ups, and gentle rotations.

Scene 1 – Opening Hook:
Show a lipstick package against an elegant background.
Text: "Your Next Beauty Inspiration"

Scene 2 – Product Reveal:
Reveal the lipstick with a smooth close-up shot.

Scene 3 – Product Details:
Show the packaging and lipstick in detail.

Scene 4 – Lifestyle Presentation:
Arrange the lipstick with makeup accessories.

Scene 5 – Final Product Shot:
Display the product against a minimal background.

Scene 6 – Call to Action:
Show the brand name "VELVET GLOW" and the text "Discover Your Shade."

## Requirements
- Maintain consistent product packaging.
- Use realistic proportions and soft lighting.
- Keep text readable.
- Use smooth transitions.
- Avoid random logos and distorted objects.
- Do not include fake customer testimonials.
- Do not make unsupported product claims.

## Expected Output
A short-form lipstick advertising video or a storyboard that can be used to generate individual video clips.
